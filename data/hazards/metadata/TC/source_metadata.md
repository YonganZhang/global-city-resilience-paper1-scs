## Tropical Cyclone Wind CC - 100 Years

The tropical cyclone wind map indicates the mean value of wind velocity for a return period of 100 years. The tropical cyclone events used for the elaboration of this map consider climate change based on models representative concentration pathways (RCP) scenarios. The map has global coverage and a resolution of 6&#039; (~11.1 kilometers at the equator).
