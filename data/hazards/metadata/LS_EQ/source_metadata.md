## Susceptibility Class of Landslides Triggered By Earthquakes

 The earthquake-induced landslides susceptibility map classifies the terrain into five susceptibility classes by combining slope, vegetation, lithology, and soil moisture information from global datasets. The weights of different susceptibility factors were calibrated to the information available in landslide inventories and physical processes. The susceptibility map has global coverage and a resolution of 3  arc-sec. (~90 meters at the equator). The data is available as 5-degree x 5-degree tiles (6000 pixels x 6000 pixels), compressed into 30-degree x 30-degree packages.
